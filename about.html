About Ramey & O'Neil

Curtis Ramey

Co-Founder | Ramey & O'Neil Custom Builders

With over 25 years of experience in the homebuilding industry, Curtis Ramey brings a deep commitment to craftsmanship, efficiency, and industry leading execution in every project he leads. His journey began humbly and hands-on—spending four formative years framing custom homes alongside skilled Amish frame carpenters. That early exposure to quality and precision laid the foundation for his approach to building.

Curtis later spent a decade in leadership roles overseeing construction and regional operations for Fortune 500 homebuilding companies. During this time, he honed his expertise in large-scale project management, operational efficiency, and team development—skills he now applies to custom residential builds with an unwavering focus on detail and elevated client experience.

Today, Curtis leads with a focus on operational excellence and a tireless pursuit of perfection in every home Ramey & O'Neil builds. He believes in the power of long-standing relationships—with trade partners, suppliers, and clients—and leverages those trusted connections to ensure seamless collaboration and superior outcomes.

At the heart of Curtis's work is a passion for turning vision into reality—creating beautifully crafted homes that stand the test of time.

Casey O'Neil

Co-Founder | Ramey & O'Neil Custom Builders

Casey O'Neil is a seasoned real estate and homebuilding professional with over two decades of expertise in delivering high-quality, production neighborhoods for the largest homebuilders and luxury custom homes for the most discerning clients. As the co-founder of Ramey & O'Neil, Casey has mastered the art of blending the efficiency and scale of production homebuilding with the tailored craftsmanship of luxury custom residences, creating communities and homes that elevate the standard of living in the greater San Antonio market.

Casey's career began in land acquisition and development, where he honed his ability to identify prime opportunities and navigate complex entitlement processes. Working for a leading national homebuilding company, he played a pivotal role in securing and developing key parcels that fueled the company's growth in San Antonio. This experience laid the foundation for Casey's entrepreneurial vision, leading him to establish his own land acquisition, entitlement, and development company. The company successfully transformed raw land into thriving residential communities, showcasing his strategic foresight and deep market knowledge.

Today, Casey O'Neil is recognized for his unique ability to bridge the gap between large-scale production homebuilding and the artistry of luxury custom homes. His hands-on approach, combined with a commitment to innovation and quality, ensures that every project reflects excellence, whether it's a master-planned or community or an exclusive custom estate.

Our team is made up of licensed professionals, skilled tradespeople, and design specialists who work together to bring your vision to life—on time and within budget.
